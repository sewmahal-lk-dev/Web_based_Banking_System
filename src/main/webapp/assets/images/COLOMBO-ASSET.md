# Homepage skyline image

Add the licensed photograph as `src/main/webapp/assets/images/colombo-lotus.jpg`.

Use a Colombo evening/night panorama, ideally 2400 x 1400 pixels or larger, with the illuminated Lotus Tower in the rightmost quarter and its crown in the upper third. Leave the left half relatively quiet for text. Warm red, wine and gold illumination will suit the design. Keep the tower clear of the central card presentation.

`public.css` places this image behind the hero, with responsive positioning and theme-aware overlays. Until supplied, the wine/champagne gradient is the intentional fallback. No substitute city image or CSS tower is used. Check the final photograph at desktop and mobile widths after adding it.
